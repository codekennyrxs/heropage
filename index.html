Inventory & stock

Never lose track of a single item

Every product, price, and count in one place. Kahvi uses FIFO cost batches — so when you restock at a new price, your profit stays accurate to the exact peso. Low-stock badges tell you what to buy before you run out.

  • Live stock counts updated with every sale
  • Low-stock alerts so hindi ka na maubusan
  • FIFO cost batches for true, accurate margins
  • Bulk import via CSV — dump your whole list in minutes

Offline-first · yours to keep

Gawa para sa totoong Pilipinong tindahan

Most POS apps die the moment the internet drops. Kahvi runs entirely on your own computer — brownout, walang load, walang signal, tuloy pa rin ang benta. When you're online, you get the extras: a hourly sales digest on your phone, remote viewing, and cloud backup. But you never depend on them.

  • Fully offline at the counter — always
  • Remote phone access to peek anytime you're connected
  • Runs on modest hardware — no need for a bagong PC
  • Automatic backups so your data is safe
